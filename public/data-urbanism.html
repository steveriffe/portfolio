Bridging Civic Architecture & Enterprise Data Systems

Speaker's Introduction & Origins

Invited to speak on my background and philosophy, I wanted to examine the through-line connecting my two careers: Architecture and Enterprise Data.

The built environment solved this roadmap long ago. Working alongside Dale Sinclair during his landmark updates to the RIBA Plan of Work, I watched architectural practice evolve from fragmented linear handoffs into an outcome-driven lifecycle. That framework became the blueprint I've relied on ever since to build load-bearing data systems.

In laying out this philosophy, I wanted to spotlight some of the extraordinary built environment projects I supported along the way.

Architectural Blueprint

The 8 RIBA Stages Applied to Enterprise Analytics

How structural civic architecture informs every phase of the modern data engineering lifecycle.

STAGE 00 Strategic Definition
Fixed Units of Truth

Resolve fundamental contradictions, core business questions, metric grains, and regulatory boundaries before drawing code. Balance executive vision with computational reality.

STAGE 01 Preparation & Brief
Heritage Audit & Discovery

Survey legacy data stores, undocumented schemas, and unmaintained ETL pipelines beneath the floorboards. Audit data lineage and assess structural capacity before designing new marts.

STAGE 02 Concept Design
Parametric Modeling & Logic

Translate ambiguous stakeholder desires and organic business curves into rigorous, buildable dimensional models and deterministic mathematical SQL formulas.

STAGE 03 Spatial Coordination
Logical Schemas & Clash Detection

Construct unified logical models and detect semantic clashes across cross-functional domains (e.g., Sales vs. Finance ARR) long before code is shipped to production.

STAGE 04 Technical Design
Hardened Physical Blueprints

Lock down deterministic SQL, row-level security policies, idempotent ETL/ELT pipelines, API contracts, and SLA baselines to ensure zero operational downtime.

STAGE 05 Manufacturing & Construction
Prefabrication & DfMA Supply Chain

Design for Manufacture and Assembly. Replace bespoke artisanal reporting by assembling reusable, certified enterprise dimensions and automated semantic components.

STAGE 06 Handover
Operational Readiness & Runbooks

Hand over living, active operating systems complete with real-time telemetry, automated alerting, comprehensive runbooks, and cohort training for self-sufficient ownership.

STAGE 07 In Use
Post-Occupancy Telemetry

Continuous evaluation of voluntary user adoption, query compute costs, and latency SLAs. Operational feedback loops directly inform subsequent Stage 0 scoping.
